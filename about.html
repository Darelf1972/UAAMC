About Us

A catalyst for sustainable agricultural growth

Bridging investment capital, technical expertise, and government programs into bankable, community-driven development.

United Agri-Agra & Minerals Corp. (UAAMC) is a Philippine corporation dedicated to the development, financing, implementation, and management of agricultural, agribusiness, agro-industrial, mineral resource, and rural enterprise projects.

The company bridges investment capital, technical expertise, government programs, and private-sector opportunities to create impactful, bankable development initiatives — supporting farming communities, cooperatives, local government units, and private investors alike.

UAAMC is positioned to support projects aligned with national development priorities and Agri-Agra financing programs, contributing to agricultural productivity, technology transfer, employment generation, and countryside development.

UAAMC operates with a strategic focus on agricultural modernization, value-chain development, food security enhancement, rural industrialization, and resource-based enterprises that generate inclusive economic growth for farming communities, cooperatives, local government units, and private investors.

Vision

To become a leading Philippine agribusiness and development enterprise that transforms agricultural resources into sustainable economic opportunities while empowering rural communities and contributing to national food security.

Mission

  • Enhance agricultural productivity and competitiveness
  • Promote sustainable investments in rural communities
  • Generate employment and livelihood opportunities
  • Strengthen food security and value-chain integration
  • Foster strategic partnerships among government, investors, and local stakeholders
