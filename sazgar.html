UAAMC Corporate Partners

Engineering mobility for practical, inclusive transport

UAAMC presents SAZGAR Philippines Inc. as one of UAAMC’s corporate partners. SAZGAR Engineering Works Limited was established in 1991 and developed into a three-wheeler vehicle and automotive wheel-rim manufacturer. The company has more than 1,300 employees, more than 33 years of experience, presence in more than 30 global markets, and revenue exceeding 57 billion.

SAZGAR Philippines Inc. is a Philippine corporation, with an address in the U.P. Laguna International Industrial Park, Barangay Mamplasan, Biñan, Laguna.
