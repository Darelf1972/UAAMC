UAAMC presents SAZGAR Philippines, Inc. as a corporate partner. Based on the supplied brochure, SAZGAR Engineering Works Limited was established in 1991 and developed into a three-wheeler vehicle and automotive wheel-rim manufacturer. The brochure describes more than 1,300 employees, more than 33 years of experience, more than 30 global markets, and revenue exceeding 57 billion.

SAZGAR Philippines, Inc. is identified in the brochure as the Philippine entity, with an address in the U.P. Laguna International Industrial Park, Barangay Mamplasan, Biñan, Laguna.
