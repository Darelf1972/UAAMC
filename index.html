About Us

A catalyst for sustainable agricultural growth

United Agri-Agra & Minerals Corp. (UAAMC) is a Philippine corporation dedicated to the development, financing, implementation, and management of agricultural, agribusiness, agro-industrial, mineral resource, and rural enterprise projects — bridging investment capital, technical expertise, and government programs into bankable development initiatives.
